# TAFE examination environments

The catalog offers one OpenSAT Digital SAT simulation and one IELTS entry. The existing IELTS Reading practice remains available until an administrator publishes the full IELTS Academic form. Archived records and previous attempts retain their IDs.

In the visual test editor, create IELTS Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking source sets. Assign them in IELTS Academic management and upload authentic, rights-cleared Listening audio. Publication requires 40 keyed questions for Listening and Reading, two Writing prompts, and three Speaking prompts in order. The Reading set starts preselected. Default section times are 30, 60, 60 and 14 minutes.

Listening and Reading scores use a TAFE practice conversion. Writing and Speaking are scored manually by administrators. Overall becomes available after all four bands are present. Speaking recordings are private to the learner and authorized administrators.

SAT uses the blue module workspace and scientific tools. IELTS has section navigation, an independent audio player, adjustable Reading columns, an essay editor and a recording panel. Selected text opens a viewport-clamped toolbar with yellow, mint and lavender options. The toolbar transitions from 0 to 100% opacity and from 3px below its final position over 140 ms ease-out. Reduced motion disables that animation.
